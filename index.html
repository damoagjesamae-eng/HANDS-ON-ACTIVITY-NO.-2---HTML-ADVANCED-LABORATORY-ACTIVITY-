About the Event 💻

Welcome to SMCC-CCIS Week 2025, a celebration of creativity, innovation, and technological excellence. This event brings together students, instructors, and technology enthusiasts to share their knowledge and demonstrate their skills.

Join us for exciting competitions, informative discussions, technology demonstrations, and student project presentations. Discover new ideas, develop your skills, and connect with fellow students.

Our Goal: To encourage creativity, teamwork, critical thinking, and responsible use of technology among CCIS students.

Event Highlights

  • 💻 Coding Hackathon and Programming Challenges
  • 🧠 Technology Quiz Bowl
  • 🤖 Robotics and IoT Demonstrations
  • 🔐 Cybersecurity Awareness Activities
  • 🚀 Student Project Showcase
  • 🏆 Awarding Ceremony and Recognition

Example 4: URL Encoding

The code %20 represents a space in a URL. For example, "CCIS Week" becomes "CCIS%20Week". The ampersand (&) separates query parameters.

Example 5: HTML vs XHTML

HTML:

<img src="logo.png" alt="Event Logo">

XHTML:

<img src="logo.png" alt="Event Logo" />

In XHTML, elements must be properly closed and empty elements such as images use a closing slash. HTML5 allows the image element without the slash.
